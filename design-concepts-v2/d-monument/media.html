A newspaper, a radio station and an agency.

The Group publishes the country’s first independent daily colour newspaper, runs its first commercial private radio station, and owns a full-service marketing agency that works for companies, government and non-profits.

TODAY in Seychelles is also published in a digital edition, eTODAY, alongside the quarterly marine magazine SEALIFE and the Sunday magazine OZORDI Dimans.
