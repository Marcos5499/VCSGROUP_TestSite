Our story

12companies in the Group today

Since 1991

The VCS Group began on 2 December 1991, when Marc Houareau incorporated Victoria Computer Services as the first computer services company in Seychelles. VCS went on to develop the first software suite used in the country’s tourism industry, and helped bring the internet to Seychelles through its subsidiary, Atlas Seychelles Limited.

Over three decades the Group grew by founding and acquiring companies. Today it has twelve companies and nearly 300 people, working in eight sectors of the Seychelles economy, and represents some of the world’s best-known brands, from BMW and Honda to Microsoft, Cisco, Xerox, Bacardi and Diageo.

Its shareholders also hold investments in cornerstone national enterprises, including H. Savy Insurance, Seychelles Breweries, Cable & Wireless Seychelles and Seychelles Commercial Bank.

The journey

1991

Victoria Computer Services is founded, the first computer services company in Seychelles.

2001

The Group acquires Honda (Seychelles).

2004

Executive Motors introduces BMW to Seychelles. SeaShell Travel begins operating.

2005

Switch is founded as a full-service marketing agency.

2007

Savy & Sons is founded. Chevrolet joins Executive Motors.

2008

Executive Cars Rental is incorporated.

2009

Crown Motors brings the first GWM cars to Seychelles.

2010

Printhouse is set up at Providence on 1 November.

2011

TODAY in Seychelles launches on 1 March, the first independent daily colour newspaper.

2012

Pure 90.7 FM goes on air in August, the first commercial private radio station. Crown Motors becomes the Peugeot dealer.

2017

Executive Motors becomes the authorised Isuzu Truck dealer.

2021

Today Publishers launches the Sunday magazine OZORDI Dimans.

2023

Crown Motors becomes the official HAVAL distributor. BMW electric vehicles arrive.
