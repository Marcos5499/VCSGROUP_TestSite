The VCS Group began on 2 December 1991, when Marc Houareau incorporated Victoria Computer Services as the first computer services company in Seychelles. VCS went on to develop the first software suite used in the country’s tourism industry, and helped bring the internet to Seychelles through its subsidiary, Atlas Seychelles Limited. Over three decades the Group grew by founding and acquiring companies.
