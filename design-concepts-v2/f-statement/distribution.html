DISTRIBUTION

Savy & Sons is the leading beverage wholesaler in Seychelles.

It imports wines, spirits and beverages and supplies the country’s major hotels, restaurants, bars and shops.

Its origins go back to the 1930s and Harry Savy & Company, one of the first import and wholesale trading houses in Seychelles. Savy & Sons itself was founded in 2007.

  • Import of wines, spirits and beverages
  • Wholesale to hotels, restaurants and bars
  • Retail supply
  • Distribution on Mahé, Praslin and La Digue
