ICT

Victoria Computer Services is the leading IT company in Seychelles.

It supplies software, hardware and IT services to banks, hotels, airlines, restaurants, corporate service providers, schools, government ministries, parastatals and small businesses, and keeps them running with local support.

Companies depend on IT more than ever. VCS helps them choose the right technology, protect what they have already invested and get support they can rely on.
