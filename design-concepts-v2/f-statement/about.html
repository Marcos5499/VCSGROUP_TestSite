OUR STORY

Founded byMarc Houareauon 2 December1991 atLe Chantier.

2 December 1991 · Le Chantier, Victoria

Today the Group has twelve companies and nearly 300 people in eight sectors.

The VCS Group began on 2 December 1991, when Marc Houareau incorporated Victoria Computer Services as the first computer services company in Seychelles. VCS went on to develop the first software suite used in the country’s tourism industry, and helped bring the internet to Seychelles through its subsidiary, Atlas Seychelles Limited.

Its shareholders also hold investments in cornerstone national enterprises, including H. Savy Insurance, Seychelles Breweries, Cable & Wireless Seychelles and Seychelles Commercial Bank.

The journey

1991Victoria Computer Services is founded, the first computer services company in Seychelles.
2001The Group acquires Honda (Seychelles).
2004Executive Motors introduces BMW to Seychelles. SeaShell Travel begins operating.
2005Switch is founded as a full-service marketing agency.
2007Savy & Sons is founded. Chevrolet joins Executive Motors.

2017Executive Motors becomes the authorised Isuzu Truck dealer.
2021Today Publishers launches the Sunday magazine OZORDI Dimans.
2023Crown Motors becomes the official HAVAL distributor. BMW electric vehicles arrive.
