PRINTING

Printhouse is the largest privately owned printing firm in Seychelles.

It runs Heidelberg offset colour presses alongside Xerox and Mimaki digital presses, so it can handle long runs and short, fast jobs in one place.

Printhouse was the first in Seychelles to print with chemical-free computer-to-plate technology, and it prints MICR cheque books for the Central Bank of Seychelles and every private bank in the country. iMedia publishes the official Seychelles directory.

  • Offset colour printing
  • Short-run digital printing
  • Security printing and MICR cheques
  • Stationery and large format
  • Directories and publishing
