Travel & Tourism. By air, by road and at sea.

TOURISM

Three companies look after travellers to and from Seychelles: an IATA-accredited travel agency, a chauffeur and car rental company, and a sport-fishing and cruising charter on board the Island Star.

SeaShell Travel is regularly ranked by the major airlines as one of the best-performing agencies in the country, and offers its service 24 hours a day.

  • Air ticketing for every airline serving Seychelles
  • Chauffeur-driven limousine service
  • Private transfers and self-drive rental
  • Corporate fleet leasing
  • Sport fishing and coastal cruising
