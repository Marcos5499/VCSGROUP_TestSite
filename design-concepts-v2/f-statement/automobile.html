AUTOMOBILE

The Group sells and services Honda, BMW, Chevrolet, Isuzu, Peugeot, GWM and HAVAL vehicles in Seychelles.

Its technicians are factory trained and work with the computer-based diagnostic systems of every brand it represents.

Sales, service and spare parts sit together at the Autoland Complex on Avenue d’Arhoa, Providence Industrial Estate.

  • New vehicle sales
  • Authorised after-sales service
  • Genuine spare parts
  • Computer-based diagnostics
  • Honda motorcycles and power products
