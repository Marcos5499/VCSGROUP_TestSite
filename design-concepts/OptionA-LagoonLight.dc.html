About the Group

One group. Eight sectors. A single commitment to Seychelles.

Founded by Executive Chairman Marc Houareau as the country’s very first computer services company, VCS pioneered local IT and helped bring the internet to Seychelles. Through diversification and acquisitions, the Group has grown into a foundational pillar of the nation’s modernisation and economic growth.
