Three directions for vcs.sc

Each option is a full, scrollable home page. All three use the same content, logo and brand palette. They differ in mood, layout and motion, so the Chairman can choose a direction rather than a colour.

The logo teal (#3790AB) is kept for large shapes and highlights. White text on teal buttons uses the deeper Action Teal (#23748E) so it stays readable. Body text is navy or slate, never pure black.

OPTION A Lagoon Light Type: Manrope

Bright, airy and corporate, following Calendly: a floating pill navigation, soft brand-colour shapes behind images, and division cards that expand on hover over one shared texture. It also has a click-through testimonial carousel.

OPTION B Deep Ocean Type: Outfit

Immersive and cinematic, following ER Group: full-screen image overlays, the profile's cover ribbon sweeping in as you scroll, and division panels that stack over each other. Light sections are mixed in so the page stays readable.

OPTION C Heritage & Horizon Cormorant Garamond + Manrope

Editorial and confident, combining both references. Serif headlines echo the “GROUP” wordmark, alongside a bento grid of divisions, an interactive sector index, a horizontal journey timeline and a navy feature band.

How to review
  • Open any option full-window (or press Play) and scroll. Shapes, images and text animate in as you go, and the navigation floats above the page.
  • Hover over the division cards, click the testimonial arrows or the cards on either side, and press buttons to feel the click response.
  • Leave a comment on anything the Chairman likes or dislikes. Sections can be mixed across options.
  • Image areas marked “Photo” are ready for VCS’s own photography: Autoland, Printhouse, Island Star, the VCS Building and so on.
